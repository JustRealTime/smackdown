# Third-party components

- **PeerJS** 1.5.5 (MIT), `tools/peerjs.min.js`, license in `tools/LICENSE-peerjs.txt`. Used for peer-to-peer lobbies; the public PeerJS broker only introduces browsers to each other.
- **Lilita One**, **Nunito**, **JetBrains Mono** (SIL Open Font License 1.1), `fonts/`, licenses in `fonts/LICENSE-*.txt`. Bundled, nothing is loaded from Google servers.
- Music in `music/`: MIDI files, original compositions made for this game with `tools/compose.py` and `tools/nocturne.py` (pieces in the style of other composers, no copied melodies).
- **GeneralUser GS** 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins, `music/gm.sf` (the part of the SoundFont that the songs use, cut out and compressed by `tools/sfbuild.py`). Licence: `tools/LICENSE-GeneralUser-GS.txt` (free for commercial use). **Open point for the commercial plans (ads, shop, Steam):** the author writes that some samples come from other free banks and he cannot be 100 % sure of their origin, though no complaint ever came. Before earning money with the game: decide whether that risk is acceptable, or replace the font (e.g. with samples recorded or bought with a clear licence).
