    Touch
    Bump into someone to start a typing duel

    Green ring with +2 next to a name: in a duel you would type 2 words fewer than them. Red with −2: 2 words more.

    Snacks give less and less as you level up, and you can be caught while eating. Win a duel and you get all of your opponent's levels. Both players start from the same phrase length: the more is at stake (high levels or a big level gap), the longer it is. The player with the lower level types a few words fewer. Run away and get caught, and your opponent gets a shorter one.

    Touch: drag anywhere to walk, Dash and Run buttons, tap an item to use it. Bump into someone and the keyboard pops up for the typing duel. iPhone fullscreen: Share → Add to Home Screen.
